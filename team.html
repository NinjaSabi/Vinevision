The People Behind the Practice

Two Experts. One Shared Purpose.

Every programme is designed and delivered by Nicole or Samantha. No juniors. No templates. Just genuine expertise, full presence, and a commitment to making a real difference.

Nicole Marshall

Founder · Learning & Leadership Specialist · Facilitator · Coach

UCT Executive Coach Crucial Learning® Five Lens Enneagram Skills Dev. Facilitator

Hi, I'm Nicole — a certified coach, seasoned training facilitator and learning and development strategist passionate about seeing people become the most fruitful version of themselves.

I launched Vine & Vision rooted in a belief that true, lasting growth happens when we're connected to purpose, guided by vision and grounded in something greater than ourselves.

Over the years, I've worked across industries to support people and teams through change, growth and leadership challenges. My approach combines a deep curiosity about how people work with a focus on uncovering root causes and designing learning experiences that create real, measurable impact.

I have a unique gift for making complex concepts click — and for understanding the deeper psychology of what actually drives sustainable behaviour change. Whether it's leadership development, culture change or skills training, I help organisations create learning that doesn't just inform people, but transforms how they think and act.

Warmth. Honesty. Action. Clients say I bring presence, momentum and just the right amount of challenge to help them grow.

Samantha Thomas

Facilitator · PhD Psychology · People Development Specialist

PhD Psychology Insights Discovery EQ Specialist Academic & Industry

Hi, I'm Samantha — a people development specialist with a PhD in Psychology and extensive experience across both academia and industry. I'm passionate about designing and facilitating learning experiences that are engaging, practical and genuinely transformative.

I'm guided by the principle that "work is for man, not man for work" — I believe work should be a meaningful expression of purpose, dignity and contribution. That philosophy shapes every programme I design and facilitate.

My work is rooted in a deep understanding of how people learn, behave and adapt. I bring together psychological rigour and real-world practicality — programmes that are evidence-based but never dry, challenging but always safe.

I specialise in Insights Discovery, a 3-part Emotional Intelligence journey and programmes designed to shift "nice cultures" into genuinely high-performance environments. And yes — I've been told I'm also very funny.
