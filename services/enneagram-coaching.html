Enneagram Coaching & Team Workshops

A deeply practical framework for self-awareness, leadership growth and team dynamics — one that creates lasting change because it gets to the root of how people are wired.

The Approach

Understanding Why People Do What They Do

The Enneagram isn't a personality label — it's a map for understanding the deeper motivations, fears and patterns that drive behaviour. When people genuinely understand themselves, they make better decisions, lead more effectively and build stronger relationships.

Who This Is For

Leaders who want genuine self-awareness. Teams that know they could work better together. Organisations investing in long-term leadership development. Individuals seeking clarity about their patterns, motivations and growth edges.

What's Included

Programme Options

All programmes are available in-person across South Africa and online globally. We customise to your context.

Individual Profiles & Debriefing

A full Five Lens Enneagram profile with a 60–90 minute 1:1 debrief. Clients consistently describe this as one of the most insightful experiences they've had about themselves — and how they show up at work.

Enneagram-Based Coaching

Ongoing 1:1 coaching using the Enneagram as the foundation. Ideal for leaders who want to understand their patterns, develop their blind spots and grow their effectiveness.

Team Discovery Workshops

A facilitated team session using each member's profile to build understanding, reduce friction and improve how the team works together. Powerful for new teams and teams stuck in unhelpful dynamics.

Leadership Development via Enneagram

Integrating Enneagram insights into broader leadership programmes — helping leaders understand how their type shows up under stress, in conflict and when making decisions.
