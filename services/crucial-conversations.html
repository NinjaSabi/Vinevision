Crucial Conversations® Training

The globally proven communication skills programme that teaches people to hold high-stakes conversations with safety, skill and confidence — and get real results from them.

The Approach

The Conversations Holding Your Organisation Back

Research shows the biggest performance problems — poor results, broken trust, missed deadlines, high turnover — are almost always preceded by conversations people avoided or handled badly. Crucial Conversations® gives teams the skills and shared language to change that.

Who This Is For

Management teams where accountability is inconsistent. Organisations where difficult conversations get avoided or mishandled. HR and L&D teams wanting a shared language for communication. Any team ready to stop having the same painful conversations on repeat.

Programme Options

All programmes are available in-person across South Africa and online globally. We customise to your context.

Crucial Conversations® for Mastering Dialogue

The flagship programme. Teaches 9 evidence-based skills for navigating high-stakes conversations — when opinions vary, emotions run strong and the outcome really matters. Grounded in 30 years of social science.

Crucial Conversations® for Accountability

Specifically designed for managers and leaders. How to discuss disappointing performance, broken commitments and poor behaviour — in a way that builds relationships rather than destroying them.

Crucial Influence®

A framework for understanding and changing the six sources of influence that drive human behaviour. For leaders who want to create lasting behaviour change, not just short-term compliance.
