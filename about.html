The Name, The Vision

Why Vine & Vision?

The name is rooted in John 15: "I am the vine, you are the branches… apart from Me, you can do nothing." It captures a core belief — that true, lasting growth happens when we're connected to purpose, guided by vision and grounded in something greater than ourselves.

For us, that translates into everything we do. We don't chase quick fixes. We work with organisations and individuals who are ready to do the real work — the kind that creates genuine, measurable change.

Vine & Vision was founded by Nicole Marshall in 2025 after more than 15 years building leadership capability across corporate South Africa. The vision from the beginning was a boutique practice where every client gets real expertise, full attention and a tailored approach — not a consultant with a template.

How We Work

Our Philosophy

There's a reason most training doesn't stick. It focuses on content delivery instead of the deeper question: what actually drives sustainable behaviour change?

◈

Root Cause First

We don't design before we diagnose. Every engagement starts with understanding the real challenge — not the presenting symptom. This takes more time upfront and saves enormous time later.

◎

Design for Transfer

We ask one question throughout: will this change how people behave on Monday morning? If not, we redesign it. Learning that stays in the room is not learning — it's entertainment.

❋

Partners, Not Vendors

We work closely alongside your people, not at arm's length. You'll always work directly with Nicole or Samantha — people who've held leadership roles themselves and understand the complexity.
