Our Story

Built on Purpose. Delivered with Heart.

Vine & Vision was founded on a simple belief: that when people grow, organisations thrive. We bring together deep expertise in learning strategy, psychology and leadership — and we work where those things intersect.

We're a boutique practice. That means every engagement gets Nicole and Samantha's full attention, not a junior consultant with a template. We partner with you to understand the real challenge before we design anything.

Based in Durban, we work across South Africa and globally online — with organisations who are serious about developing their people.
